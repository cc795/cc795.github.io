Hello, and welcome to my personal website!

I am `Can Cui`, an Assistant Professor at the Institute of Deep Space Exploration, Nanjing University. 

I received my bachelor's degree from Pennsylvania State University (2010–2014), my master's degree from the Institute of Astronomy, University of Cambridge (2014–2015), and my Ph.D. from the Shanghai Astronomical Observatory (2016–2020). I subsequently held postdoctoral positions at the Department of Applied Mathematics and Theoretical Physics (DAMTP), University of Cambridge (2020–2023) and the University of Toronto (2023–2024).

My research focuses on `protoplanetary disks` and `planet formation`, combining `numerical simulations` and `analytical theory` to investigate the physical processes that govern the formation and evolution of planetary systems.
