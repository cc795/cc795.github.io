课程简介Course overview

  • 已发现的系外行星已有6000余颗（NASA Exoplanet Archive）。
  • 因此，行星形成是一门被观测推着走的理论。
  • 过去十五年里，ALMA、JWST 和凌星巡天把这门学科从少数几个约束条件，推到了必须对上千个系统负责的地步。
  • 本课程按照物理过程组织：先建立盘的结构与演化，再处理固体的输运与聚集，最后才谈行星本身的生长、迁移与系统结构。

    • Planet formation is a theory course driven by observation. In the last fifteen years ALMA, JWST and the transit surveys have taken the subject from a handful of constraints to a field answerable to thousands of systems. The course is organised by physical process rather than by phenomenon: first the structure and evolution of the gas disk, then the transport and concentration of solids, and only then the growth, migration and final architecture of the planets themselves.

    大纲Syllabus

    L01

    导论与观测背景Introduction and observational context

    课程安排。理论必须解释的两组数据：太阳系结构与陨石年代学，以及系外行星统计。YSO 分类与 αIR、吸积率、盘的寿命。尘埃连续谱与分子线诊断。第一批物理：竖直静力平衡、h = cs/Ω、亚开普勒转动、最小质量太阳星云、时标层级。

    Logistics. The two datasets a theory must explain: Solar System architecture with meteoritic chronology, and exoplanet demographics. YSO classification and αIR, accretion rates, disk lifetimes. Dust continuum and molecular-line diagnostics. First physics: vertical hydrostatic equilibrium, h = cs/Ω, sub-Keplerian rotation, the MMSN, the hierarchy of timescales.

    Q1 · 气体盘的演化Q1 · Evolution of the gas disk
    L02

    盘的结构 I —— 竖直结构与温度Disk structure I — vertical and thermal

    静力平衡与高斯剖面；自引力与磁压带来的偏离。径向力平衡与竖直剪切。被动辐照盘：外张、Chiang–Goldreich 双层模型、过热表面层。粘滞加热盘。作为热力学地标的雪线。

    Hydrostatic equilibrium and the Gaussian profile; departures from it through self-gravity and magnetic pressure. Radial force balance and vertical shear. Passive irradiated disks: flaring, the Chiang–Goldreich two-layer model, superheated surface layers. Viscously heated disks. Ice lines as thermodynamic landmarks.

    A20 §2.2–2.4
    SF §3.1–3.2
    L03

    盘的结构 II —— 不透明度、电离与化学Disk structure II — opacity, ionization, chemistry

    尘埃与气体的不透明度。热电离；宇宙线、X 射线与放射性核素的非热电离。电离度与气体–磁场的耦合。盘化学概览：冻结、雪线、C/O 比，以及 ALMA 和 JWST 实际测到的是什么。

    Dust and gas opacities. Thermal ionization; non-thermal ionization by cosmic rays, X-rays and radionuclides. Ionization fraction and the coupling of gas to magnetic fields. Disk chemistry in outline: freeze-out, snow lines, C/O ratios, and what ALMA and JWST actually measure.

    SF §3.3
    L04

    盘的演化 I —— 经典粘滞盘Disk evolution I — the classical viscous disk

    把角动量输运写成有效粘滞。推导 Σ(r,t) 的扩散方程；格林函数解与自相似解；粘滞扩展与适用范围。α 参数化。边界条件：零力矩内边界、磁层截断、双星中的盘。粘滞加热与竖直温度结构。

    Angular momentum transport as an effective viscosity. Derivation of the diffusion equation for Σ(r,t); Green's-function and self-similar solutions; viscous spreading and the limits of validity. The α-prescription. Boundary conditions: the zero-torque inner boundary, magnetospheric truncation, disks in binaries. Viscous heating and the vertical temperature structure.

    A20 §3.1–3.2
    SF §4.1–4.3
    L05

    盘的演化 II —— 输运的起源Disk evolution II — the origin of transport

    为什么分子粘滞不够。流体力学路线：线性稳定性、Rayleigh 判据、VSI / COS / SBI 与 zombie vortex。自引力、Toomre 判据、引力湍流与碎裂。磁转动不稳定性：局域分析、增长率与饱和。

    Why molecular viscosity fails. Hydrodynamic routes: linear stability, the Rayleigh criterion, VSI / COS / SBI and the zombie-vortex instability. Self-gravity, the Toomre criterion, gravito-turbulence and fragmentation. The magnetorotational instability: local analysis, growth rates, saturation.

    A20 §3.3
    SF §5.1–5.4
    L06

    非理想 MHD、盘风与间歇吸积Non-ideal MHD, disk winds and episodic accretion

    欧姆、双极与 Hall 三个区域，以及各自在盘中的位置。死区及其后果。磁离心风与磁热风；磁场的输运。间歇吸积：FU Ori 与 EX Lup 爆发、S 曲线、热不稳定与死区不稳定。

    Ohmic, ambipolar and Hall regimes, and where each dominates in a protoplanetary disk. The dead zone and its consequences. Magnetocentrifugal and magnetothermal winds; magnetic-field transport. Episodic accretion: FU Ori and EX Lup outbursts, the S-curve, thermal and dead-zone instabilities.

    SF §5.4–5.5, §6
    Q2 · 固体的输运与聚集Q2 · Transport and concentration of solids
    L07

    固体的空气动力学Aerodynamics of solids

    曳力区（Epstein、Stokes）与 Stokes 数。竖直沉降与沉降–搅动平衡。径向漂移：Weidenschilling 问题与漂移壁垒。湍流扩散；Nakagawa–Sekiya–Hayashi 平衡解。颗粒堆积。漂移与颗粒生长的观测特征。

    Drag regimes (Epstein, Stokes) and the Stokes number. Vertical settling and the settling–stirring balance. Radial drift: the Weidenschilling problem and the drift barrier. Turbulent diffusion; the Nakagawa–Sekiya–Hayashi equilibrium. Particle pile-ups. Observational signatures of drift and of grain growth.

    A20 §4.1–4.2
    SF §7.1–7.2
    L08

    星子的形成Planetesimal formation

    碰撞凝聚；弹跳与碎裂壁垒。压强极大与粒子陷阱；纬向流、涡旋与 Rossby 波不稳定性。流动不稳定性：物理机制、线性判据与数值结果。卵石云的引力坍缩与初始星子质量函数；与小行星带和柯伊伯带的比较。

    Collisional coagulation; the bouncing and fragmentation barriers. Pressure maxima and particle traps; zonal flows, vortices and the Rossby-wave instability. The streaming instability: mechanism, linear criterion, numerical results. Gravitational collapse of pebble clouds and the initial planetesimal mass function; comparison with the asteroid and Kuiper belts.

    A20 §4.3–4.5
    SF §7.3, §8
    Q3 · 从星子到行星系统Q3 · From planetesimals to planetary systems
    L09

    从星子到原行星From planetesimals to protoplanets

    引力聚焦与带弥散的二体问题。失控生长与寡头生长。粘滞搅动与动力学摩擦。孤立质量。N 体方法及其局限。晚期阶段：巨撞击、月球形成事件，以及 Hf–W 定年给出的时标。

    Gravitational focusing and the two-body problem with velocity dispersion. Runaway and oligarchic growth. Viscous stirring and dynamical friction. The isolation mass. N-body methods and their limitations. The late stage: giant impacts, the Moon-forming event, and the timescale from Hf–W chronometry.

    A20 §5.1–5.3
    L10

    卵石吸积Pebble accretion

    气动耦合固体的吸积：Bondi 与 Hill 区、吸积率、卵石隔离质量。与星子吸积的比较。全局卵石流与受漂移限制的核生长。对木星形成与太阳系同位素二分性的意义。

    Accretion of aerodynamically coupled solids: the Bondi and Hill regimes, accretion rates, the pebble isolation mass. Comparison with planetesimal accretion. Global pebble flux and drift-limited core growth. Implications for the formation of Jupiter and for the Solar System's isotopic dichotomy.

    A20 §5.4
    近期文献current literature
    L11

    巨行星的形成Giant planet formation

    核吸积：吸积包层的结构、临界核质量、Kelvin–Helmholtz 阶段与失控气体吸积。时标困难及其解决方案。引力不稳定路线：冷却判据、碎裂半径，以及它能与不能产生什么。二者与观测到的巨行星族的对照。

    Core accretion: the structure of an accreting envelope, the critical core mass, the Kelvin–Helmholtz phase and runaway gas accretion. Timescale problems and proposed solutions. The gravitational-instability route: cooling criteria, fragmentation radii, and what GI can and cannot produce. Confronting both with the observed giant-planet population.

    A20 §6.1–6.3
    L12

    行星–盘相互作用与迁移Planet–disk interaction and migration

    共振力矩：Lindblad 与共转。I 型迁移及其符号；饱和、热力矩与动力学力矩。开缝与 II 型迁移。III 型／失控迁移。偏心率与倾角的阻尼。共振俘获与共振链；用迁移解释热木星、共振超级地球系统与盘的子结构。

    Resonant torques: Lindblad and corotation. Type I migration and its sign; saturation, thermal and dynamical torques. Gap opening and Type II migration. Type III / runaway migration. Eccentricity and inclination damping. Resonant capture and chains; migration as an explanation for hot Jupiters, resonant super-Earth systems, and disk substructure.

    A20 §7.1–7.3
    SF §8
    L13

    系统结构、盘的消散与综合System architectures, dispersal and synthesis

    盘的消散：光致蒸发（EUV/FUV/X 射线）与 MHD 风；双时标演化与过渡盘。盘后演化：动力学不稳定、高偏心迁移、潮汐。大气逃逸与半径谷。碎屑盘。族群合成：现有模型能重现什么，又在哪里失败。开放问题与未来十年的数据。

    Disk dispersal: photoevaporation (EUV/FUV/X-ray) and MHD winds; two-timescale evolution and transition disks. Post-disk evolution: dynamical instability, high-eccentricity migration, tides. Atmospheric mass loss and the radius valley. Debris disks. Population synthesis: what current models reproduce and where they fail. Open problems and the next decade of data.
